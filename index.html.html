The Community Rule

Compiled by Watchman Dominic Ezekiel Managed, Sponsored, and Powered by Prayer Union of Africa

Chapter 1: The Foundation of the Covenant

1 This is the book for the whole way of life of the community. It is for those who want to seek Our Father In Heaven with all their heart and soul. 2 They must do what is

good and right before Him, just as He commanded through Moses and all His servants, the prophets.

3 They must love everything that He chooses and hate everything that He rejects. 4 They must stay far away from all evil and hold tightly to every good deed. 5 They must practice truth, righteousness, and justice on the earth, and they must no longer walk in the stubbornness of a guilty heart and eyes full of lust.

6 For these wrong ways lead only to doing all kinds of evil and satisfying sinful desires. 7 All those who dedicate themselves to His truth must be brought into the covenant of Our Father In Heaven. 8 This is so they can walk perfectly before Him and follow everything that has been revealed for their times.

9 They must love all the children of light, each according to his place in the community of Our Father In Heaven. 10 But they must hate all the children of darkness, each according to his guilt, because they belong to the company of the angel of darkness (Satan).

11 Everyone who joins the truth of Our Father In Heaven must bring all their knowledge, their strength, and their property into the community. 12 They must not hold anything back. This way, their knowledge can be purified by the truth of His laws, their strength can be guided by His perfect ways, and all their property can be managed according to His righteous plan.

13 They must not turn away from any of the commands of Our Father In Heaven, and they must not step out of their proper time. 14 They must not advance their times, nor delay any of their set feasts. 15 They must not turn aside from His true laws, neither to the right hand nor to the left.

16 And all who enter the order of the community must pass into the covenant before Our Father In Heaven. 17 They must promise to act according to everything that He has commanded, and they must not turn back because of any fear, terror, or trial that may come during the rule of Belial (Satan).

18 When they enter the covenant, the priests and the Levites must bless Our Father In Heaven, who is the source of all salvation, and praise all His faithful works. 19 Then all those who are entering the covenant must say, "Amen! Amen!"

Chapter 2: The Covenant of Renewal and Order

1 And after them, all those who enter the covenant must confess and say, "We have wandered! We have disobeyed! We and our fathers before us have sinned and done wicked things by walking against the truth and righteousness of Our Father In Heaven."

2 Then they will confess their sins before Our Father In Heaven, and they will admit that He has judged us and our fathers fairly. 3 But Our Father In Heaven, in His great mercy, has provided for us and will preserve us from all evil.

4 Then the priests shall bless all the men who belong to the lot of Our Father In Heaven, who walk perfectly in all His ways. 5 And the Levites shall curse all the men who belong to the lot of Belial (Satan). 6 They shall speak and say, "Be cursed because of all your guilty wickedness! May Our Father In Heaven deliver you up for torture at the hands of vengeful destroyers!

7 May Our Father In Heaven visit you with destruction by the hands of all those who carry out His justice! 8 Be cursed without mercy because of the darkness of your deeds! Be condemned in the shadowy place of everlasting fire! 9 May Our Father In Heaven not listen when you call to Him, and may He not forgive you by wiping away your sin!

10 May Our Father In Heaven raise His angry face toward you for justice! May there be no peace for you in the mouths of those who hold fast to the fathers!" 11 And all those who enter the covenant shall answer after them, "Amen! Amen!"

12 Then the priests and the Levites shall continue, saying: "Cursed be the man who enters this covenant while still walking among the idols of his heart! 13 He sets up his own stumbling-block of sin before him so that he may backslide. 14 When he hears the words of this covenant, he will bless himself in his heart, saying, 'I will have peace, even though I walk in the stubbornness of my heart.'

15 But his spirit will be swept away, both the thirsty and the satisfied, leaving nothing behind. 16 All the curses of this covenant shall cling to him, and Our Father In Heaven will set him apart for evil. 17 He shall be cut off from the midst of all the holy children of light. 18 Because he has turned aside from Our Father In Heaven on account of his idols and his stumbling-block of sin, his lot shall be among those who are cursed forever."

19 And all those who enter the covenant shall answer after them, "Amen! Amen!"

20 This is how they shall do it, year by year, for as long as the rule of Belial (Satan) endures. 21 The priests shall enter first, ranked one after another according to the

perfection of their spirit. 22 Then the Levites, and thirdly, all the people, one after another, in their groups of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens.

23 This is so every person may know his place in the community of Our Father In Heaven according to the everlasting design. 24 No man shall move down from his place, nor move up from his assigned position. 25 For according to the holy design, they shall all be in a community of truth and humble virtue, of kindness and good intent one toward another, and they shall all be children of the everlasting company.

Chapter 3: The Two Spirits and the Nature of Man

1 The leader of all the children of righteousness is the Angel of Light, but the leader of all the children of darkness is the Spirit of Darkness (Satan). 2 All who walk in the ways of light are under the influence of the Prince of Light, and they walk in the ways of truth. 3 All who walk in the ways of darkness are under the influence of the Angel of Darkness (Satan), and they walk in the ways of error.

4 It is the Angel of Darkness (Satan) who leads all the children of righteousness into error. All their sins, their wicked deeds, their faults, and their lawless acts are under his dominion, according to the mysteries of Our Father In Heaven. 5 This continues until the time He has appointed for judgment.

6 However, Our Father In Heaven, in His perfect wisdom, has ordained a time to put an end to all falsehood. 7 At that time, He will cleanse all the deeds of truth on the earth and destroy the spirit of darkness (Satan) forever. 8 Truth will then rise up throughout the world until the time of the final judgment.

9 Until that day, these two spirits fight against each other in the heart of every person. People walk in either wisdom or folly. 10 If a person loves light, all his deeds will be righteous. If he loves darkness (Satan), all his deeds will be evil. 11 For Our Father In Heaven has created these two spirits to exist together until the final appointed time.

12 He knows every action they will take, and He has allotted them as a portion for every man. 13 No one can change their path, for everything is held in the hand of Our Father In Heaven until the time of His visitation.

14 Blessed are all those who walk in the spirit of truth. They are purified from all their sins and uncleanness by the holy spirit. 15 They receive the gift of knowledge through the truth of Our Father In Heaven, and they are cleansed from their guilt by the spirit of holiness. 16 By this truth, they are set apart as His holy children.

17 But those who refuse to follow the laws of Our Father In Heaven cannot be purified. 18 They are trapped in their own uncleanness and wallow in the filth of their rebellion. 19 They will not be cleansed by any water of purification, nor can they be made holy by any ritual washings. 20 As long as they reject the truth of Our Father In Heaven, they remain in their state of error, and their judgment will be heavy and final.

Chapter 4: The Teachings for the Children of Light

1 This is the instruction for all the members of the community regarding the spirits of mankind. It explains how people are divided by their nature, their deeds, and their final reward. 2 Our Father In Heaven created the two spirits to be equal until the end of time, and He placed them in the hearts of men. 3 One spirit leads to truth and righteousness, while the other leads to darkness and perversion.

4 The nature of the spirit of truth is to show kindness, patience, deep love, and wisdom that is grounded in the understanding of Our Father In Heaven. 5 It produces faith in all the works of God and trust in His great compassion. 6 It also brings forth a spirit of knowledge and a humble, pure heart. 7 A person filled with this spirit shows mercy to all who walk in the truth, maintains a spirit of holiness, and speaks the truth in love.

8 The spirit of darkness (Satan), however, leads to greed and laziness in doing what is right. 9 It promotes wickedness, pride, lying, and deceit. 10 It is characterized by cruelty, a lack of judgment, and a heart that is far from Our Father In Heaven. 11 This spirit also breeds jealousy, arrogance, and a desire to spread evil. 12 It walks in the path of darkness (Satan) and acts with a spirit of rebellion against the light.

13 Our Father In Heaven has made these two spirits enemies of each other. 14 Their ways are complete opposites, and they are constantly at war. 15 All those who love the truth will live under the light of His blessing, and their reward will be peace and long

life. 16 But those who follow the spirit of darkness (Satan) will find only bitterness and destruction. 17 They will suffer the eternal judgment of Our Father In Heaven because they chose to walk in the ways of evil.

18 The community must understand these things so they can test every person's spirit. 19 Each member must be judged according to the spirit that rules their heart, for Our Father In Heaven sees the truth of every soul. 20 Those who hold to the truth will be gathered into His light, while those who follow the spirit of darkness (Satan) will be cut off from His people forever.

We have completed Chapter 4. There are 7 chapters remaining. Shall we proceed to Chapter 5?

Chapter 5: Establishing the Order of the Community

1 All those who dedicate themselves to the truth of Our Father In Heaven must live according to the laws of the community. 2 They must separate themselves from the men of perversion who walk in the way of wickedness. 3 They must not associate with them in their work or their property, so they do not become defiled by their guilty ways.

4 Every member must follow the instructions of the sons of Zadok—the priests who guard the covenant—and the majority of the men of the community. 5 By their word, the lot will be decided in every matter concerning the law, the property, and the judgment of the community. 6 They must act with truth, humility, righteousness, and pure love toward each other.

7 No one should hide their knowledge or their property from the community. 8 If anyone is found to be acting deceitfully regarding his property, he will be penalized and excluded from the pure meals of the community for a set time. 9 If he persists in his rebellion, he will be judged according to his stubbornness of heart.

10 When a person enters the covenant to join the community, he must promise to turn away from all evil and to walk according to all the commands of Our Father In Heaven. 11 He must promise not to act according to his own desires or the ways of the world. 12 He must submit to the authority of the community and the teachings of the law, for this is the way to be kept safe from the influence of the spirit of darkness (Satan).

13 The priests are to record the names of the brothers, one after another, according to their rank. 14 Each person must know his place and his duty. 15 They must watch over one another in love and truth, ensuring that no one falls back into the ways of darkness

(Satan). 16 If any man speaks against the laws of the community or spreads division, he will be brought before the members and warned. 17 If he refuses to listen, he will be removed from among the children of light, for he has chosen the path of the wicked.

Chapter 6: Rules for Gathering and Conduct

1 This is the rule for the gathering of the community. Every member, from the least to the greatest, must meet together to discuss matters of the law and the welfare of the brotherhood. 2 When they are gathered, ten men must always be present to ensure that the law of Our Father In Heaven is studied and interpreted faithfully. 3 The priest must preside over the meeting, and every person shall speak only when it is his turn, according to his rank.

4 No one shall interrupt another, nor shall they speak about foolish things that do not build up the community. 5 When they sit to eat their bread or drink their wine, they must do so in order, with the priest blessing the food first. 6 By this, they acknowledge that the blessing comes from Our Father In Heaven.

7 If any member acts against the rules of the community, he shall be judged by the council of the members. 8 If a man is found lying about property or holding back his resources, he shall be fined or excluded from the community meal for a time. 9 This is necessary to purify the group from the influence of the spirit of darkness (Satan).

10 Every member must be tested annually. The priests and the leading men shall examine the spirit and the deeds of every individual. 11 If a man has walked in the truth and followed the commands, he shall be moved up in rank. 12 If he has failed or acted in pride, his rank shall be lowered, or he shall be removed, for he has allowed the spirit of darkness (Satan) to take root in his heart.

13 The members must keep their hearts and spirits pure. 14 They must always be ready to do the will of Our Father In Heaven. 15 They shall not harbor hatred or resentment against a brother, but shall correct one another in love and humility. 16 By living this way, they remain a light in the world, prepared for the day of final salvation

Chapter 7: Discipline and Corrections

1 These are the rules for disciplining those who fail to follow the way of the community. If any member acts against the laws of Our Father In Heaven or speaks disrespectfully to his brother, he shall be corrected according to the severity of his actions. 2 If a man speaks in anger or lies to his neighbor, he shall be excluded from the common meal for ten days.

3 If a man interrupts another when he is speaking, he shall be silenced for ten days as a sign of humility. 4 If a man sleeps while the community is gathered for study, he shall be corrected for thirty days. 5 If a man leaves the meeting without permission, he shall be corrected for one day. 6 If he does this three times, he shall be excluded for a longer period to see if he truly seeks to repent and walk in the light.

7 If a man harbors hatred in his heart against his brother, he must be confronted. 8 If he refuses to confess his fault and repent, he brings upon himself the guilt of the spirit of darkness (Satan). 9 The community must then judge him, for he has chosen to separate himself from the truth of Our Father In Heaven.

10 No member shall accuse another without the witness of at least two others who have seen the offense. 11 This is to ensure that truth is maintained and that no one is unjustly punished. 12 If a man is found guilty of spreading rumors or causing division, he shall be cast out, for he is a source of confusion among the children of light.

13 Everyone must understand that these disciplines are not meant to destroy, but to refine the soul. 14 Just as gold is purified by fire, the heart of the believer is purified by the correction of the community. 15 By accepting this, they remain faithful to Our Father In Heaven and keep themselves ready for the day of His visitation.

Chapter 8: The Council of the Community

1 Within the community, there shall be twelve men and three priests who are perfect in all that has been revealed to them. 2 They shall practice truth, righteousness, justice, love, and humility toward one another. 3 They shall preserve the earth through their faithfulness and atonement for sin, and they shall carry out the judgment of Our Father In Heaven.

4 When these men are gathered together, they shall form a council to seek the truth. 5 They shall be the foundation of the holy house of Our Father In Heaven. 6 They shall be a safe place for the children of light, a place where the truth is upheld and where the spirit of darkness (Satan) has no power.

7 If any matter arises in the community, this council shall seek the instruction of Our Father In Heaven. 8 They shall not act according to their own understanding, but according to what has been revealed in the law. 9 By doing this, they keep the community pure and united.

10 They shall walk in perfection before one another in all their dealings. 11 They must be men who are chosen by their spirit, not by their status. 12 Every member of this council must be tested to ensure that his heart is fully surrendered to the service of Our Father In Heaven.

13 If they follow these ways, they will be a witness of the truth to the entire world. 14 Their lives will reflect the light of Our Father In Heaven, and they will be established as a true community of believers. 15 They shall await the time when Our Father In Heaven will visit the earth to establish His kingdom forever, and they shall be ready, for their hearts are blameless and full of truth.

Chapter 9: The Way of Perfect Knowledge

1 These are the ordinances for the men of the community who choose to walk in the way of perfection. 2 They must separate themselves from all those who have turned aside from the way of truth. 3 They shall devote themselves to studying the law and following the guidance of Our Father In Heaven.

4 They shall be the witnesses of righteousness, proving the truth through their deeds. 5 Their speech shall be measured, and their actions shall be guided by the light of holiness. 6 They must not speak of the secret things of the law to those who walk in the ways of darkness (Satan), but shall keep them among the faithful.

7 If a man seeks to join this path, he must bring all his knowledge and strength into the community. 8 He must show by his life that he has abandoned the ways of the world and is fully committed to the path of Our Father In Heaven. 9 If his heart is found to be double-minded, he shall not be accepted, for his spirit is still influenced by the spirit of darkness (Satan).

10 The members of the council shall examine every person who comes forward. 11 They shall look for humility, a love for truth, and a hatred for all evil. 12 If he proves himself faithful, he shall be brought into the covenant and taught the mysteries that Our Father In Heaven has revealed.

13 They shall live together in harmony, sharing their resources and their lives. 14 By this, they demonstrate that they are a people set apart for Our Father In Heaven. 15 They shall wait patiently for the time when He will make His truth known to all, and in that day, they will be found blameless, having lived according to His perfect will.

Chapter 10: The Times and Seasons of Praise

1 At the beginning of the day and at its end, I will sing of the wonders of Our Father In Heaven. 2 I will praise Him for all His works, for the order He has set in the stars and the seasons. 3 When the day begins, I will bless Him, and when the night falls, I will speak of His faithfulness.

4 I will declare that the hand of Our Father In Heaven sustains all things. 5 From the rising of the sun to its setting, His glory is over all the earth. 6 Even when the spirit of darkness (Satan) tries to bring confusion, I will fix my eyes on the truth of His law. 7 My lips shall offer a sacrifice of praise, for this is the fruit of a heart that is surrendered to Him.

8 I will bless Him when I go out and when I come in, when I sit and when I rise. 9 I will not allow my mouth to be filled with vanity or lies, for I belong to the community of

truth. 10 My life shall be a constant prayer, a song that rises to the ears of Our Father In Heaven.

11 I will honor the times He has appointed for feasts and holy gatherings. 12 I will not delay, and I will not rush, but I will walk in the order He has established. 13 Everything I do shall be according to His plan, and I will seek His guidance in every thought and action.

14 In this way, I will keep myself from the traps of the wicked and the influence of the spirit of darkness (Satan). 15 I will dwell in the shelter of the Most High, and my soul will be at peace, resting in the truth that Our Father In Heaven is my reward forever.

Chapter 11: The Hymn of Final Deliverance

1 I belong to Our Father In Heaven, and all my salvation comes from Him. 2 He is the source of all my knowledge, and He has established my path in righteousness. 3 Although the spirit of darkness (Satan) surrounds me, I will not be afraid, for the power of Our Father In Heaven is with me always.

4 He has placed my feet on solid ground, and He has opened my eyes to see the wonders of His truth. 5 Even when I am weak, His strength is my defense. 6 He has purified me with His holy spirit, and He has set me apart to be a vessel of His glory.

7 I will not trust in my own understanding, nor in the wisdom of men. 8 I will look to Our Father In Heaven, for He knows the secrets of the heart and the future of all things. 9 He has delivered my soul from the pit of destruction, and He has granted me a place among the children of light.

10 Blessed be the name of Our Father In Heaven, for He is just and merciful. 11 He will judge the wicked and reward those who walk in His way. 12 Though the spirit of darkness (Satan) seeks to deceive, the light of His truth will never be extinguished.

13 I will walk in this truth until the end of my days. 14 I will remain faithful, knowing that my labor in the community is not in vain. 15 When the time comes for Him to visit the earth, I will stand before Him with a heart that is blameless, having served Him with all my strength, and I will dwell in His presence forever.

Commentary: The Call to Holiness in the Last Days

We are living in the final days, a time when the distinction between the holy and the unholy is becoming increasingly clear. The Word of God does not expire; it remains as

authoritative today as it was at the beginning. Just as it was written then, so it stands now: the divide between those who walk in the light and those who dwell in darkness is final.

The model for our community is found in the early days after the ascension of Jesus. When the Holy Spirit descended upon the believers, they were united in one heart and one mind. In that holy fellowship, no one lacked anything because they held all things in common, surrendered fully to the will of the Lord.

However, we must remember the gravity of this walk. The story of Ananias and Sapphira serves as a stern warning to all who would claim to be part of the community while harboring deceit in their hearts. When they lied to the Holy Spirit and stole from the community of believers, they were judged instantly. Their fate reminds us that the presence of the Holy Spirit is not a matter to be trifled with; it is a consuming fire that demands total integrity.

A Warning to the Reader Do not seek to join this community if you are not prepared to follow the scriptures completely or if you are not ready to live a full-time, uncompromising holy life. This is not a social gathering; it is a covenant walk. While we live in modern times, it is not difficult for a true believer to fully surrender to the Lord if their heart is genuinely seeking His truth.

Book 2: The Damascus ScripturesChapter 1: The Call to Remember and the Warning against Rebellion

1 Listen now, all you who know righteousness, and understand the works of Our Father In Heaven. For He has a controversy with all flesh and will execute judgment upon all who despise Him. 2 When Israel turned away and became unfaithful, He hid His face from the world (Motherland) and from His sanctuary, and He delivered them to the sword. 3 Yet, when He remembered the covenant He made with our ancestors, He left a remnant for Israel and did not give them up to be destroyed.

4 In the age of wrath, three hundred and ninety years after He gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, He visited them. 5 He caused a root of planting to spring forth from Israel and Aaron to inherit His land and to prosper on the good things of the earth. 6 They perceived their guilt and recognized that they were like blind men, groping for the way for twenty years.

7 Then Our Father In Heaven observed their deeds, that they sought Him with a whole heart, and He raised up for them a Teacher of Righteousness to guide them in the way of His heart. 8 He made known to the later generations what He did to the generation of old, the company of those who turned aside, and those who followed them. 9 For they walked in the stubbornness of their hearts, and they were caught in the nets of the spirit of darkness (Satan).

10 Therefore, hear me, all you who have entered the New Covenant in the land of Damascus: 11 You must separate yourselves from the way of the wicked. 12 Do not defile yourselves with their impurities, for their way is a path of destruction. 13 Whoever enters this covenant must swear to return to the law of Moses with all his heart and soul, and to do nothing that is contrary to the truth of Our Father In Heaven.

14 You are to love your brother as yourself, to support the poor, the needy, and the stranger. 15 Do not hold a grudge against your neighbor, and do not seek revenge. 16 Let every man rebuke his neighbor in truth and not be guilty of sin because of him. 17 Walk in perfection in all your dealings, for those who do so will inherit eternal life, and all the glory of Adam will be theirs.

18 This is the mandate for all who dwell in the camps: 19 Let there be an overseer in every camp to instruct the many in the works of Our Father In Heaven. 20 He shall examine them in the laws, heal their spirits, and lead them in righteousness, so that they may be ready for the day when He shall visit the earth (Motherland) to set all things right.

Chapter 2: The Three Nets of the spirit of darkness (Satan)

1 And now, listen to me, all you who have entered the covenant, and I will open your eyes to see the ways of Our Father In Heaven. 2 Understand that the spirit of darkness (Satan) has set three nets in the world (Motherland) to ensnare the children of light. 3 He has laid these traps from of old: the first is fornication, the second is riches, and the third is the defilement of the sanctuary.

4 Whoever escapes one is caught in another, and whoever escapes that one is caught in the third. 5 Through these, the people have been led astray, thinking they were following the truth while their hearts were entangled in the corruption of the world (Motherland). 6 They have taken wives in fornication, and they have gathered riches that do not satisfy, and they have polluted the holy place where the name of Our Father In Heaven dwells.

7 But the wise among you shall perceive the traps and avoid them. 8 For how can a man serve Our Father In Heaven while his heart is occupied by the desires of the flesh or the greed of wealth? 9 These things are contrary to the covenant, and they separate the soul from the light of life.

10 Do not be like those who claim to know the truth but act as if they are blind. 11 You must keep yourselves pure, for the eye of Our Father In Heaven is upon all your works. 12 He searches the heart and tests the spirit of every man to see if he is truly for Him or for the spirit of darkness (Satan).

13 Remember those who were judged in the past for their rebellion and their refusal to heed the warning. 14 They were consumed by their own choices, for they would not turn back to the path of righteousness. 15 You, however, have been called to be a holy nation, a people set apart for His glory. 16 Walk, therefore, in the statutes I have set before you, and do not deviate to the right or to the left, that you may be preserved in the day of final visitation.

14 The priests shall preserve the knowledge of these days and shall guard their proper order.

15 The people shall not follow confusion concerning the times of gathering, but shall remain faithful to the order that has been revealed.

Chapter 24The Sacred Calendar, the Counting of Days, and the Order of the Seasons

1 These are the commands concerning the appointed order of days, months, and seasons, so that the people may walk according to the times established before Our Father in Heaven.

2 The year shall follow its appointed course, and the people shall recognize the seasons according to the order placed in creation.

3 The appointed days shall be observed at their proper times, and no one shall alter the order of the sacred calendar.

4 The priests shall preserve the knowledge of the times and shall teach the people when the holy gatherings shall take place.

5 The seasons shall come according to their appointed arrangement, each fulfilling the purpose for which it was established.

6 The people shall not confuse the times of holiness, nor shall they replace the appointed order with human traditions.

7 The counting of days shall be preserved carefully, so that the festivals and offerings may be observed at the correct times.

8 The priests shall keep watch over the calendar and shall maintain unity among the people through the proper observance of the appointed seasons.

9 The heavens shall serve as a testimony of the order established by Our Father in Heaven, for all creation follows His wisdom.

10 The sun, the seasons, and the appointed signs shall continue according to the order given from the beginning.

11 The people shall remember that every time and season has a purpose, and that holiness requires faithfulness to the appointed order.

12 The festivals shall not be moved from their proper places, and the sacred gatherings shall remain according to the covenant.

13 The priests shall teach these matters to the generations that follow, so that knowledge of the holy times is not lost.

14 The people shall prepare themselves before the appointed days arrive, entering them with gratitude and reverence.

15 The order of the year shall testify that Our Father in Heaven has established harmony in creation.

16 Those who preserve the sacred calendar shall preserve the remembrance of the covenant among the people.

17 The Temple service, the offerings, and the gatherings shall all be performed according to the appointed times.
